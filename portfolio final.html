Hello, I'm Wesley.

Product Manager at adidas Running, working across global brand collaborations and leading concepts, account partnerships and exclusives.

With a background in fashion wholesale and sports management, I bring together two perspectives in every project: the story behind it and adidas Running. My work sits where culture meets performance, from franchise selection and collection architecture to translating a story into footwear, apparel and accessories that feel true to both sides.

Product that feels true to its story, whether a partner, a retailer or a race, while staying rooted in adidas Running DNA.

02Performance first

03Commercially relevant

Product built to resonate in culture and to sell through.

Each project starts from two perspectives: the story behind it and adidas Running. Whether it is a partner's world, a retailer's community or a race moment, my role is to bring both together, shaping how they meet in product and ensuring a consistent expression from concept to final outcome.

The common thread is building product stories that connect performance running with culture in a way that feels authentic, commercially relevant and true to both sides.
